## How to demo (2 minutes)

1. Open **dashboard** on laptop → log in as **admin**  
2. On a phone: open `/order?table=3` → add items → **Dërgo porosinë**  
3. Order appears live on dashboard → **U bë**  
4. Show **QR page** (`/qr`) → print sheet for 30 tables  

---

## Tables

- 30 tables = 30 QR codes  
- Link format: `…/order?table=1` … `table=30`  
- Print once from `/qr` → stick on tables
